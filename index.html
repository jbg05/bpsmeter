Questions

What do you need from us?

A CSV or JSON export of 30 to 90 days of stablecoin payouts, in whatever format your system produces. Run the anonymizer on it first. It keeps six columns and drops the rest.

Do you hold funds or execute trades?

No. We quote, route and score. Your desk executes every trade on its own accounts and credentials.

Which corridors do you cover?

USD to EUR, MXN, BRL, COP and NGN today. Tickets in other corridors are listed in the report with what you paid, never dropped. Tell us which ones you need.

Where do your venue prices come from?

Public quotes read at several ticket sizes wherever a venue publishes them. OTC desks and off-ramps quote privately, so their prices are modeled and every figure that uses them is labeled estimate.

How do I know the report was built from my file?

The anonymizer prints a SHA-256 fingerprint of the file you send, and the report prints the fingerprint of the file it was built from. Hash your copy with shasum -a 256 and compare.

How long does a report take?

A few days from the file arriving. The first one is free.
