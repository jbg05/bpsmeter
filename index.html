How it works

From your payout history to a number you can act on.

1

Export your fills

Pull 30 to 90 days of payouts from your system and run our anonymizer on the file. It strips names, payment IDs and everything else we don't need.

2

We price every ticket

For each payment we check what the cheapest venue that could take that size would have charged. Published rates where venues publish them, clearly marked estimates where they don't, live quotes if you want them.

3

You get the number

A report with your all-in cost, the gap by corridor and provider, and the specific tickets worth moving. It's free and it's yours to keep.

For your engineers

Same engine, as an API.

Call it before a payment goes out, or send a batch of history for analysis. Available to design partners.

POST /v1/route (design partners)
# ask for the best route before a payment goes out
curl https://api.rhumbfx.com/v1/route \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $RHUMB_KEY" \
  -d '{ "corridor": "USD-COP", "notional": 250000, "deadline": "2m" }'

# returns the split, the all-in cost in bps, and the quotes behind it
{ "all_in_bps": 21.4, "vs_single_source_bps": 118.0,
  "route": [{ "venue": "circle_stablefx", "pct": 100, "quote_id": "q_9f2a" }],
  "execute_on": "your_credentials" }
Security and data

We see as little as possible.

Anonymized on your side

The report only needs corridor, size, provider and fee. Our anonymizer runs locally, removes everything else, and lists exactly what it removed before you send anything.

No custody

We never hold your money. Every route runs on venues you already use, under your own credentials.

Used once

Whatever you send is used for your report and deleted when you ask. A mutual NDA and a data processing agreement come first.

Or send nothing

If you'd rather not share a file at all, share your screen for fifteen minutes and we'll run it live.
