Questions

What do you need from us?

A CSV or JSON export of 30 to 90 days of stablecoin payouts, run through the anonymizer first.

Do you hold funds or execute trades?

No. We quote, route and score. Your desk executes on its own accounts.

Which corridors do you cover?

USD to EUR, MXN, BRL, COP and NGN. Tickets in other corridors are listed in the report, never dropped.

Where do venue prices come from?

Public quotes read at several ticket sizes. Private OTC and off-ramp prices are modeled and labeled estimate.

How do I know the report used my file?

Compare the SHA-256 the anonymizer prints with the one in your report, or run shasum -a 256 yourself.

How long does it take?

A few days from receiving the file.
