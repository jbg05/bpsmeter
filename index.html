Questions

Who is BPSMeter for?

Payment companies, payout platforms and remittance firms that convert stablecoins into local currency, especially across more than one provider.

Which corridors do you cover?

USD to EUR, MXN, BRL, COP and NGN today. Payouts in other corridors are still listed in your report, never dropped.

Where do the venue prices come from?

Public quotes read at several ticket sizes wherever a venue publishes them, including exchange order books and on-chain aggregators. OTC desks and off-ramps quote privately, so their prices are modeled and marked as estimates.

Is our file encrypted?

The anonymizer removes everything except six columns, then computes a SHA-256 hash of the file. The hash is a fingerprint, not encryption: it proves which file a report was built from. For transfer we use whatever secure channel you prefer, or a screen share where nothing is sent.

Do you execute or hold funds?

No. BPSMeter is analytics software. Your team executes every trade on its own accounts.

How long does a report take?

A few days from receiving the file.
