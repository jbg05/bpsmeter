What happens to your data

We only ever see the six columns the analysis needs.

It runs on your machine

The anonymizer is a small script with no dependencies and no network calls. You can read it before you run it.

Every file fingerprinted

The anonymizer prints a SHA-256 of the file you send, and your report prints the same one, so you can confirm it was built from that file.

No custody, no logins

We never hold funds, never ask for venue credentials and never place a trade. Your desk executes everything.

Used once

Your file is used for your report and deleted on request, under a mutual NDA and a data processing agreement.

Questions

Who is BPSMeter for?

Payment companies, payout platforms and remittance firms that convert stablecoins into local currency, especially across more than one provider.

Which corridors do you cover?

USD to EUR, MXN, BRL, COP and NGN today. Payouts in other corridors are still listed in your report, never dropped.

Where do the venue prices come from?

Public quotes read at several ticket sizes wherever a venue publishes them, including exchange order books and on-chain aggregators. OTC desks and off-ramps quote privately, so their prices are modeled and clearly marked as estimates.

Do you execute or hold funds?

No. BPSMeter is analytics software. We quote, route and score, and your team executes on its own accounts.

What does it cost?

The first cost report is free. After that, a routing sheet rebuilt each month on fresh quotes, priced on volume.
